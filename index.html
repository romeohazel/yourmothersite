How it works.

  1. 1.

    Tell it which sites are actually work.

    GitHub, Notion, Linear, Docs — whatever you're supposed to be on. Type, hit enter, done.

  2. 2.

    Try to slip away.

    The way you always do. "Just one tweet." "Just ten seconds of TikTok." You know exactly who you are.

  3. 3.

    Hear from your mother.

    A random clip starts playing. Then another. No repeats in a row. She doesn't stop until you go back to work.
